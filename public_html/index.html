Privacy Policy

How AiratTop collects, uses, and protects information across airat.top services.

Airat.Top Blog Terms

This Privacy Policy explains how AiratTop (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use any website or service under the airat.top domain, including websites, tools, APIs, and other current or future subdomains such as about.airat.top, blog.airat.top, donate.airat.top, privacy.airat.top, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

Last updated: 10 April 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to all visitors and users of the Services. By accessing or using any Service, you agree that your information will be handled as described here and in our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Technical data: IP address, browser user agent, device type, operating system, and referring URL, captured in standard server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Usage analytics: Aggregated metrics such as page views, session duration, approximate geography, device information, click and navigation patterns, and similar on-site interaction data collected via Google Analytics and Yandex.Metrica.
  • Voluntary communications: Information you provide when contacting us (for example, email address and message content).
  • Donation transparency: Cryptocurrency wallet addresses are published for voluntary contributions; we do not collect sender details beyond what is publicly available on the corresponding blockchain.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Services may use first-party cookies and browser storage to remember preferences (such as language or theme). Google Analytics and Yandex.Metrica may set cookies or use similar technologies to gather usage statistics and measure interactions with the Services. You can restrict cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect certain functionality.

4. Use of Analytics Services

We use Google Analytics and Yandex.Metrica to understand how visitors interact with the Services. These providers may process technical identifiers, cookie data, page visit information, approximate location, referrer data, and interaction events. Where enabled, Yandex.Metrica may also provide session replay-style interaction review tools such as Webvisor. Google Analytics processes data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Opt-out Browser Add-on. Yandex.Metrica processes data as described in Yandex’s Privacy Policy.

For Yandex.Metrica, we enable the privacy setting that prevents storage of visitors’ full IP addresses.

We use these tools for aggregate reporting, traffic measurement, troubleshooting, and product improvement. We do not use analytics data to directly identify individual users.

5. How We Use Information

  • Maintain and improve the Services, including performance and stability monitoring.
  • Analyse aggregate usage trends to inform future product decisions.
  • Respond to support or partnership inquiries you submit.
  • Protect against malicious or unauthorized activity.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers necessary to operate the Services (e.g., hosting, analytics), all bound by contractual obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of AiratTop, our users, or the public.

7. Data Retention

Server logs are typically retained for up to 90 days unless longer storage is needed for security investigations. Analytics data is retained according to the configuration and retention schedules of the relevant analytics provider. Communication records are kept for as long as necessary to respond and maintain a clear history.

8. International Visitors

By using the Services, you understand that information may be processed in jurisdictions where we or our service providers operate, which may have data protection laws different from those in your country.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Services after changes becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email mail@airat.top.
